Howie Roseman

Gallery

Before/During Kelly

Roseman was initially an Eagles intern in 2000, but climbed the ladder to become the general manager in 2010. Even though Roseman had his title, Andy Reid still had more control of the roster.

In 2013, Jeffery Lurie hired Chip Kelly as the head coach, and he also had a lot of control over the roster. In their first season together, the team won 10 games, but later the team would struggle.

Post-Kelly

In 2016, when Chip Kelly was replaced by Doug Pederson, Roseman had much more control of the roster and draft.

2 years later, the Eagles won Super Bowl LII and Roseman was promoted again.

With Nick Sirianni a few years later, the Eagles went to Super Bowl LVII and LIX, winning LIX 40-22.
